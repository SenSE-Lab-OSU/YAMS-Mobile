Contact

Email chang.1560@osu.edu. Please include your phone model, its operating system version, and the app version shown in your device’s app settings. If a collection session behaved unexpectedly, the session folder name is the most useful thing you can send with it.

What you need

  • MotionSenSE wristbands. The app collects from this research hardware and cannot record without it.
  • iOS 15.1 or later, or Android 7.0 or later.
  • Bluetooth turned on, and Bluetooth permission granted to the app.

Trying the app without a wristband

Open the ☰ menu at the top right and turn on Simulated device. A stand-in wristband appears in the scan list and the whole workflow can be followed from there. Its data is not real, and any session it takes part in is saved to a folder whose name begins with DEMO-.

Where session files are saved

  • Android: the phone’s Downloads/yams-mobile-data folder, reachable from any file manager.
  • iOS: the app’s folder in the Files app, under On My iPhone › YAMS Mobile › yams-mobile-data.

Each session is one folder, containing a text file per wristband plus a session_info.json recording the subject and session numbers.

Common questions

Why does the app need Bluetooth? Bluetooth is how it finds wristbands, connects to them, and receives their timing signal. It is never used to determine your location.

Can I lock the phone during a session? Yes. Collection continues with the screen off. On Android a notification stays in the drawer for as long as a session is running, so you can confirm at a glance that recording has not stopped.

A wristband disconnected mid-session. Did I lose the session? No. The app reconnects automatically and resumes recording to the same files. The sample counter in each file shows exactly where any interruption occurred.

Does any data leave the phone? No. The app has no accounts and makes no network connections. Files stay on the device until research staff copy them off.
